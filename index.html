How to play

  1. Tap (or press Space), every tap is one step for your hamster.
  2. The ghost replays Squeektoshi's real run: he speeds up, hesitates and pauses exactly when he did on the wheel.
  3. Mind your stamina: mashing drains it and your steps get weaker. A steady rhythm wins races.
  4. First to complete the ghost's step count wins. Beat his actual recorded time!

Honest data note: Ghosts are replayed from GET /v1/measurement/runs on the public Squeekchain API, device-signed wheel evidence (certifiedCoreWork = false), not SqueekCore-certified work. Live status comes from GET /v1/status. Your race results live only in this browser, nothing is recorded onchain. Sample ghosts, when used, are synthetic practice data and are labelled as such. No wallet, no account, no tracking.

Squeek Sprint is an independent fan game built on Squeekchain's public data (Sepolia testnet). Not an official Squeekchain product.
